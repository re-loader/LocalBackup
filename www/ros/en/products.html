QR payment pad In development

A small touch pad for coin-operated machines - a washing machine, a water vending machine: the customer picks an amount, pays by PromptPay QR, and the pad sends pulses to the machine's coin input.

  • Three ways to sell: one price; three buttons and a typed amount; a roller of ten prices
  • The QR with a time limit; paid, the pulses counted on the screen
  • An operator page behind a long press
  • On the Guition JC3248W535 (3.5") and JC4848S040 (4")

Now: every screen of the flow, with a demo payment. Next: the payment connection, the pulse output and the settings.

APGAR timer

The newborn's APGAR timer for the delivery room. BIRTH starts the clock; the timer calls each score at its minute and keeps the record.

  • The 1, 5 and 10 minute scores - and 15 and 20 while the 5-minute score is below 7
  • A soft beep before each score, three at it; the score sheet opens by itself, even over another app
  • Appearance, Pulse, Grimace, Activity, Respiration, 0 to 2 each, the total in its colour
  • English or Thai; every birth, score and reset in a log on the SD card
  • A running session survives a restart

An app on the device's flash drive, laid out for every board's screen; made for the JC3248W535.

Health kiosk

A blood-pressure kiosk for hospitals and clinics on the 7" JC1060P470C, mounted vertically or horizontally.

  • The patient is identified with the Thai ID card or a QR code
  • Guided with pictures and voice, measured by a professional blood-pressure monitor
  • The result goes into the hospital's database (HL7 into MySQL) - only after the patient confirms
  • A slip from a receipt printer

Identify

Thai national ID card through the card reader, or a QR code on the scanner.

Send

Straight into the hospital's database, with the patient's consent first.
