Install Reload OS from the browser

Pick your board, connect it by USB and press Install: the browser writes Reload OS onto it. Nothing to download or set up - it needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux).

  1. Pick your board

    An image runs on its own board only. Over USB nothing stops the wrong one: the board then starts with a dark screen, and the right image puts it right.

    Loading the list…

  2. Connect it

    Pick a board first: which of its USB sockets to use depends on it.

    Connect the board's USB Type-C socket of the chip itself (USB Serial/JTAG) to the computer. In the browser's list it is "USB JTAG/serial debug unit".

    Connect the board's UART socket (its USB-serial chip, a CH340 or CH343) to the computer. In the browser's list it is "USB Serial"; if nothing shows, the computer needs the chip's driver - the installer links to it. Writing through this chip takes several minutes.

    A board that does not answer: hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then press Install again.

  3. Install

    This erases the whole board - the firmware it came with, its settings and its files. The firmware a board was sold with cannot be put back from here.

    This browser cannot reach a USB serial port. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. The installer works on an HTTPS page only.

After it

  • The board restarts into the home screen in about 5 seconds
  • It shows "Not activated" until it gets its product key: Settings › About
  • Its flash drive is empty: apps come from Studio or the app kit
  • Later versions: Settings › About › Check online - no cable

Good to know

  • The installer is ESP Web Tools, served from this site; its window is in English
  • The image is the whole 16 MB flash; the browser sends it compressed
  • Not on a phone or tablet, and not in Safari or Firefox: they have no Web Serial
  • See the system first: Try it runs it in the browser
